Why GD Arena?

GD Arena gives students a realistic group discussion room without requiring 6–8 people. AI participants have distinct personalities, respond to each other, and provide evidence-based feedback after the session.

Safety & transparency

The student is always informed that the other participants are AI. Microphone denial, connection failure and AI failure should fall back to typed responses/demo mode rather than breaking the session.
